The Origin Story

From Munganda to the World

Satheesh was born in Munganda village — his mother Dhanalakshmi's village, in the lush delta region of Konaseema, East Godavari. His father Satyanarayana Murthy hails from Polavaram, another Konaseema village nearby. Together, two families from this fertile river-delta land gave rise to a story that would eventually reach all of Andhra Pradesh.

Satyanarayana Murthy and Dhanalakshmi raised three children — Srinu, Satheesh, and Subhashini. The values instilled were simple but unbreakable: work hard, stand by your word, never walk away from what you started.

When debt of ₹40 lakhs came knocking and the whole family advised filing for insolvency and walking away — Satheesh refused. Those values from Munganda held firm. "I took those loans on my personal goodwill. I cannot walk away."

📍 Munganda (Mother) · Polavaram (Father) · Konaseema · East Godavari
