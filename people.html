How to Join

Four ways to engage

There is no single path into this ecosystem. Choose the level of commitment that matches where you are right now — and grow from there.

01
🙋
Volunteer
Time · Passion · Action
The entry point for most people in the ecosystem. You contribute your time and energy to community initiatives, events, and social projects. No money required — only genuine motivation to make a difference.
  • Commit to at least one ongoing initiative
  • Show up consistently, not just once
  • Bring your full skill set — not just your hands
  • Be open to growing into a bigger role
Join Vizag Volunteers →
02
🧑‍💻
Team Member
Skills · Commitment · Growth
You work inside one of the organisations — F9 Info Technologies, Kalam Dream Labs, or Vizag Volunteers. You are building something, not just supporting it. This is where careers and missions intersect.
  • Specific, demonstrable skills in your domain
  • Aligned with the mission — not just the salary
  • Ready for responsibility from day one
  • Committed for the medium to long term
Express Interest →
03
🤝
Partner
Network · Resources · Strategy
Strategic alignment with the ecosystem. You bring resources, network, or domain expertise that accelerates what we're building together. Partnerships are about mutual amplification.
  • Clear alignment on values and long-term goals
  • Something specific to bring: network, capital, expertise
  • Willing to grow together — not just transact
  • Focused on Vizag or the social entrepreneur model
Start a Conversation →
04
🌱
Ecosystem Builder
Idea · Startup · Revenue
You have a business idea and you want to build it inside this ecosystem — using the network, the platform, and the collective power of 5,000+ people to launch and grow. This is how the 100 startups vision becomes real.
  • A clear business idea that serves a real need
  • Willingness to give back to the community network
  • Entrepreneurial mindset — ownership, not employment
  • Mission-adjacent: your business should also do good

Inspiration Leaders

Shaped by Giants

Four leaders whose vision, courage, and legacy continue to drive Satheesh every single day.

01 / 04
Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam
Scientist · President · People's Leader

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts, and thoughts result in action."

Why: A child from a small village who changed a nation. Kalam taught Satheesh that origin never limits destiny — and that youth are the greatest investment. Kalam Dream Labs is Satheesh's tribute to this belief.

02 / 04
Steve Jobs
Visionary · Founder · Design Thinker

"Stay hungry, stay foolish."

Why: Jobs showed that obsession with quality, thinking differently, and refusing to accept limits could reshape entire industries. Satheesh carries the same refusal to accept "ordinary" in everything he builds.

03 / 04
Nara Chandrababu Naidu
Chief Minister · Tech Visionary · Builder of AP

"Technology is the tool of transformation."

Why: Naidu proved that governance + technology + relentless action can uplift millions. His vision for Andhra Pradesh inspires Satheesh to use digital tools to transform Vizag's communities, not just businesses.

04 / 04
Linus Torvalds
Engineer · Open Source Pioneer · Linux Creator

"Talk is cheap. Show me the code."

Why: One person, open collaboration, world-changing impact. Torvalds inspired Satheesh's belief in building transparent platforms, sharing freely, and letting the community be the force multiplier.
