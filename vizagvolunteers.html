Origin Story

Born from One WhatsApp Message

On March 27, 2020, as COVID-19 lockdowns paralysed India, Satheesh Karnatakapu sent a single WhatsApp message. Within hours, hundreds of volunteers responded. Within days, meals were being delivered to migrant workers stranded on Vizag's streets. The message had become a movement — and Vizag Volunteers Association was born.

What started as COVID relief grew into a full registered NPO. Over 4 years, volunteers delivered 2 lakh meals, organised plasma donation drives, ran 93 oxygen concentrators free of cost during the second wave, and responded as first responders to the LG Polymers gas leak disaster. From a WhatsApp group to a registered society under the AP Society Act 2001, Vizag Volunteers became the city's largest and most active citizen-led movement.

"We don't ask for money. We ask for time. Every volunteer gives their Sunday morning, their weekday evenings, their skill — and that's what changes Vizag."

— Satheesh Karnatakapu, Founder

COVID-19 Response

When Vizag Needed Us Most

During the pandemic, Vizag Volunteers mobilised thousands of citizens when the city needed them most. Volunteers ran food distribution, plasma donation drives, PPE kit distribution, and operated the Oxygen Bank — all free of cost. The District Collector issued an official appreciation letter for event management during Chandanotsavam. When the LG Polymers gas leak struck, Vizag Volunteers were among the first responders on the ground.
