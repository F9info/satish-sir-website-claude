Model 02 — Life Chapters

The Story Arc

Six chapters that shaped everything. Click any chapter to read the full story.

01
1978 – 1996
The Konaseema Boy
Born in Munganda village, East Godavari. 99/100 in Computers. Everyone else got 30–55.
Grew up in a small village in Konaseema, East Godavari. Schooled in a place far from cities and opportunities, but not far from curiosity. While classmates scored 30–55 in computers, he scored 99. Not because there was a plan — because there was a pull. That pull never stopped.

02
1997 – 2002
The Photographer
Overnight bus runs. Photo lab collections. Later, shooting from helicopters for Cairn Energy.
Ran a photo lab business in Amalapuram and Rajahmundry. Made daily overnight bus runs for two years, collecting work and delivering prints. The discipline of showing up every single day — regardless of how tired, regardless of the result — was built in this chapter. Later photographed from helicopters for Cairn Energy and Deccan Aviation. From village buses to aerial surveys.
Tap to expand ↓
03
Early 2000s
The Solar Crash
Booked 100+ solar units. Lost everything overnight. Left with ₹2,000. Refused to quit.
Traveled to Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bangalore, and Mumbai training in solar energy distribution. Became a distributor for MB Solar and booked over 100 units. Then overnight, a political contract change wiped it all out. No warning. No compensation. Left with ₹2,000 in pocket. The lesson: no system, no person, no contract is guaranteed. Build anyway.
Tap to expand ↓
04
2003 – 2007
18 Hours a Day. Zero Teacher.
Borrowed a cousin's computer. Taught himself Java, web tech, and IT business models. Bought 275+ domains before writing code.
No money for college. No internet at home. Borrowed a cousin's computer and spent 18–19 hours a day teaching himself everything — Java, HTML, web architecture, business models. Bought 275+ domains before he could build a single website. Why? Because he understood something most engineers miss: the domain is the intention. The code comes after the vision.
Tap to expand ↓
05
2008 – Present
Building F9 — The Long Game
Founded F9 Info Technologies. ₹40 lakh debt. Paid every rupee back. 600+ clients. 18 years strong.
Founded F9 Info Technologies Pvt Ltd in 2008. Built VizagPlus.com. A US client cheated the company — leaving ₹40 lakh in debt. Others might have declared bankruptcy. He refused. Paid back every rupee using a bicycle and a handmade portfolio, visiting clients door to door. That decision — to not take the easy exit — built the trust that now sustains 600+ clients and 18 years of business.
Tap to expand ↓
06
2015 – Now
The Social Fire
1,000 consecutive 5 AM mornings. 652 beach cleanups. COVID response. 5,000 volunteers. Guinness Record.
Started walking at 5 AM — 1,000 consecutive days, never missing once. Began cleaning RK Beach alone every day for 365 days. Transformed a littered viewpoint into Kalam View Point. Then COVID hit — one WhatsApp status sparked 2 lakh meals, 150+ plasma donations, bodies lifted from hospitals when no one else would go in. Today: Vizag Volunteers runs 13 projects with 5,000 members and holds a Guinness World Record. Kalam Dream Labs has trained 800+ rural students. 555 km walked to Amaravati for Safe Touch awareness. The mission is alive.

Model 04 — Wisdom & Reflections

Lessons from the Road

Not inspiration quotes. These are things that were learned the hard way — in real situations, with real consequences.

I left with ₹2,000. I had no option but to build.
Chapter 03 — The Solar Crash

After the solar distribution business collapsed overnight due to a political contract change, there was no safety net, no plan B. The constraint became the fuel.

I didn't learn from a teacher. I learned because stopping wasn't an option.
Chapter 04 — 18 Hours a Day

18–19 hours a day on a borrowed computer. No mentor, no course, no degree. Just the will to understand — and the knowledge that the city didn't owe him anything.

Others suggested bankruptcy. I chose the bicycle.
Chapter 05 — Building F9

When a US client left ₹40 lakh in debt, the easy exit was available. Choosing to pay it back — every rupee, door by door — was the decision that built everything that followed.

1,000 days. No one asked me to. No one knew.
Chapter 06 — The Social Fire

The 5 AM walks were private. No audience, no validation. Consistency without an audience is the only consistency that counts — because it proves the motivation is internal.

One WhatsApp status. That's all it took to start a movement.
Chapter 06 — COVID Response

On March 27, 2020, one post asking "I am ready to serve" sparked hundreds of volunteers. The lesson: readiness is contagious. Show it first, and others will follow.

The domain is the intention. The code comes after the vision.
Chapter 04 — The Builder

Bought 275+ domains before he could build a single website. Most people wait until they have the skills. The vision has to come first — even if the execution is years away.
