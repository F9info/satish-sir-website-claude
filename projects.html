Participation Models

Six ways to get involved

🙋

Volunteer

Give your time and skills to a social or community project. No experience needed — just commitment.

💰

Sponsor

Fund a project or event. From CSR budgets to individual giving — every contribution creates visible impact.

🤝

Collaborate

Bring your organisation's resources, reach, or expertise to co-create something bigger than either party alone.

🎓

Mentor

Share your knowledge with entrepreneurs, students, or first-time founders. An hour with the right mentor changes everything.

📣

Promote

Spread the word in your network. Referrals, introductions, and social shares are the most underrated form of support.

🚀

Build

Join as a co-founder, core team member, or early team. For projects still in formation — bring your energy and own a part of it.
