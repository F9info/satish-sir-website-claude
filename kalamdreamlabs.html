Our Story

Named After a Dreamer

Named after Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam — the People's President who believed every child deserves to dream big. Kalam Dream Labs was born to turn that belief into action. Founded in 2018 by Satheesh Karnatakapu, it bridges the gap between passionate young innovators in rural Andhra Pradesh and the real-world tools, mentors, and opportunities they deserve.

Dr. Kalam's life was itself a testament to the power of dreaming beyond one's circumstances — a small-town boy from Rameswaram who became India's missile man and, ultimately, its most beloved President. That spirit lives in every student who walks through our doors, every patent we help file, every startup we help launch. Kalam Dream Labs is more than an organisation — it is a promise to the next generation that their ambitions matter and that support is available.

"You have to dream before your dreams can come true."

— Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Social Impact

555 km Walk for Safe Touch Awareness

In one of India's most impactful awareness campaigns, Satheesh walked 555 km from Vizag to Amaravati to spread Safe Touch awareness to rural students.

555
KILOMETRES
Vizag → Amaravati

Kalam Dream Labs partnered with schools along the route to deliver sessions on child safety, digital literacy, and innovation. The walk was not just a physical journey — it was a mission to reach students who are rarely reached, in villages and small towns that national campaigns often miss.

At each stop, volunteers from the Labs conducted interactive Safe Touch workshops, teaching children about personal boundaries and helping teachers and parents build a safer environment. Over the course of the walk, hundreds of schools and thousands of students were touched by this initiative — a living example of what it means to implement global awareness for local needs.
