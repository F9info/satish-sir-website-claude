Founder & President

One WhatsApp message on March 27, 2020. Hundreds showed up. 2,09,792 lives later — it became Vizag's largest citizen-led movement. 5,000+ volunteers. 13 active projects. All powered by people who simply decided to care. "For the people. By the people. Of the people."

Vizag Volunteers

13 Projects. One Mission.

🏖️
I Love Vizag Beach
652 Sunday cleanups at Kalam View Point. Transformed a littered area into Vizag's beloved tourist spot.
🩸
Digi Blood
When a family is desperate at 2 AM and no blood bank responds, Digi Blood connects them to a willing donor within hours. Every unit given is a life extended.
📚
Skill Share
A student who knows what they're capable of is unstoppable. Skill Share delivers career counselling, industry training, and real mentorship to students who've never had access to it before.
🏥
Real Needy
Medical and economic support for genuinely needy individuals. 3 spinal surgeries funded for children.
🕯️
Jyothi Maya
Begging Free Vizag — identifying and rehabilitating beggars into government shelter systems with dignity.
💊
Project PCOD
Awareness and education for college women about Polycystic Ovarian Disorder and health management.
♿
Special People
Everyone deserves to be seen. Special People volunteers spend time with differently-abled individuals — offering companionship, practical support, and the simple dignity of being valued.
👴
Bridging Generations
Volunteers visit old age homes and orphanages — no donations, just time, presence, and smiles.
🌳
Green Vizag
A greener Vizag is a better Vizag. Volunteers plant trees and restore green spaces — protecting the natural beauty that makes this city worth fighting for, one sapling at a time.
♻️
Vizag Reuse
Collecting old clothes, books, and furniture — distributing to tribal villages including Tanda (5,000 residents).
🫁
Oxygen Bank
100 oxygen concentrators available completely free to anyone in medical need across Visakhapatnam.
🚨
SS Emergency
Rapid disaster response. First responders during the LG Polymers gas leak disaster in Vizag.
🏅
Volunteering
Vizag Marathon, Yoga Day Guinness World Record (2,500 volunteers), city events — District Collector assigned.
