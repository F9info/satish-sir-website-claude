03
Official Bios
Three lengths — use the one that fits your format

Satheesh Karnatakapu is a Visakhapatnam-based entrepreneur and social entrepreneur — founder of F9 Info Technologies, Kalam Dream Labs, and Vizag Volunteers, a movement of 5,000+ volunteers that holds a Guinness World Record.

Satheesh Karnatakapu is a Visakhapatnam-based entrepreneur, social entrepreneur, and ecosystem builder. He founded F9 Info Technologies in 2008 and has built it into an 18-year-strong IT company serving 600+ clients. Simultaneously, he founded Kalam Dream Labs to train and place rural students in technology careers, and Vizag Volunteers — a 5,000-member movement that distributed 2 lakh meals during COVID-19 and holds a Guinness World Record. Recognised with the Yogandhra 2025 Award by the Government of Andhra Pradesh, Satheesh is one of Vizag's most consistent voices for entrepreneurship, social responsibility, and community-driven change.

Satheesh Karnatakapu is a Visakhapatnam-based social entrepreneur, and community builder who has spent nearly two decades building businesses and movements that outlast any single effort. Born in Munganda village, Konaseema, he is entirely self-taught — learning technology through 18-hour days on a borrowed computer, before founding F9 Info Technologies Pvt Ltd in 2008. Today, F9 is an 18-year-old IT company with 600+ clients and a reputation for integrity that was built, literally, by paying back ₹40 lakh in debt door-to-door rather than filing for bankruptcy.

Alongside F9, Satheesh founded Kalam Dream Labs — named after Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam — to train rural students in technology and place them in IT careers. Over 800 students have been placed to date. He also founded Vizag Volunteers Association, which mobilised 5,000+ volunteers, distributed 2 lakh meals during COVID-19, facilitated 150+ plasma donations, and earned a Guinness World Record. His individual disciplines include 1,000 consecutive 5 AM morning walks and 652+ Sunday beach cleanups without missing a single week.

Satheesh is a recipient of the Yogandhra 2025 Award — conferred by the Government of Andhra Pradesh for outstanding social contribution. He is a member of CII Visakhapatnam, TiE Vizag, BNI Amigos, Lions Club, JCI Port City, YI Vizag, and a founding member of the Vizag Startup Ecosystem. His current focus is building the Dream Port platform — a city-scale talent matching engine — and a technology layer that connects Vizag's society, NGOs, government, and environment toward a Happiness Index north star.

Our next speaker is someone who needs no introduction in Visakhapatnam — but let me give you one anyway, because the numbers matter.

He founded F9 Info Technologies in 2008 and built it into an 18-year IT company with 600+ clients — not by advertising, but by showing up with integrity and delivering on every promise.
