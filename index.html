Who I Am

I started with ₹2,000 and a borrowed computer. Eighteen years later, the mission hasn't changed — use whatever I build to help people rise.

I'm Satheesh Karnatakapu, an entrepreneur and social innovator from Visakhapatnam. I run F9 Info Technologies and Kalam Dream Labs, and in 2020 I founded Vizag Volunteers — what began as a single WhatsApp message during the pandemic grew into the city's largest citizen-led movement.

Business and service have never felt separate to me. Whether it's shipping software for 600+ clients, training a student from a village for their first job, or organising a beach cleanup before dawn, it's the same instinct: show up, keep your word, and leave the place better than you found it.

Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu — Managing Director

F9 Info Technologies

Founded in 2008 in Visakhapatnam, F9 Info Technologies began as a one-man operation and has since grown into one of the city's most trusted digital solutions companies — 18 years, one honest promise at a time.

  • Evolving F9 into a next-gen AI-focused technology company, exploring automation and modern software architecture.
  • Leads as a team member, not a boss — builds a culture of shared learning and honest feedback.
  • Building KDL-OS, an open-source, click-based app builder that makes development accessible to everyone.
  • Actively mentors startups and emerging entrepreneurs, helping them turn ideas into scalable products.

Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu — Managing Director

Kalam Dream Labs

Founded in 2018 in Visakhapatnam, Kalam Dream Labs was born from Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam's philosophy — "Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts, and thoughts result in action."

  • Built an open, collaborative ecosystem of mentors, resources & opportunities for students who rarely get a foot in the door.

Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu — Founder

Vizag Volunteers

Founded on 27 March 2020 in Visakhapatnam, Vizag Volunteers Association began as a single WhatsApp message during the COVID-19 lockdown and has since grown into the city's largest citizen-led movement.

  • Registered NPO under AP Societies Registration Act, 2001 (Reg. No. 101/2021).
  • Leads by showing up: find a need, bring people together, take action.
  • Built alongside Co-founder Mr. Chandan Achary on a strong digital foundation.
  • From beach cleanups to oxygen banks, the movement keeps growing project by project.
