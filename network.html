The Vision — Network Technology

What this system should do

Managing 20,000+ relationships manually is impossible. The goal is a system that keeps every relationship warm — automatically, intelligently, with the right message at the right time.

🎯
Circle-Based Targeting
For any message, project, or event — automatically identify the relevant circle and send only to them. No noise. No mass blasts. Each person receives only what is relevant to their relationship with the mission.
Circle Filter WhatsApp Email
🎂
Relationship Warmth Engine
Never miss a birthday. Never forget an anniversary. Never lose a donor relationship to silence. Automated personal wishes — calibrated by circle, personalised by relationship, scheduled by importance.
Birthdays Anniversaries Festivals
📊
Network Analytics
Track who is active, who has drifted, which circles are growing. Identify the connectors — the people who bridge circles. Know where to invest relationship energy for maximum mission impact.
Activity Tracking Growth Metrics Connectors
🔁
Project-to-Circle Matching
When a Dream Port problem needs a biomedical engineer — the system identifies who in the network matches. When a volunteer project needs a logistics coordinator — the right circle is activated instantly.
Skill Matching Project Alerts Auto-Assign
