Our Story

Built from Zero.
Trusted by Hundreds.

What started as one man, one borrowed computer, and ₹2,000 has become one of Visakhapatnam's most trusted IT companies.

F9 Info Technologies was founded in 2008 by Satheesh Karnatakapu — not from privilege, not from capital, but from a fierce refusal to give up. Satheesh taught himself technology 18 hours a day, absorbing everything from web design to digital marketing by sheer will. He started the company with nothing more than ₹2,000, a borrowed computer, and a reputation built entirely on honesty.

In the early years, when the company faced enormous financial pressure — ₹40 lakhs of debt — Satheesh chose the harder road. He rode his bicycle to client meetings, kept every commitment, and paid back every rupee. Not because the law demanded it, but because his word demanded it.

Today, 18 years on, F9 serves 600+ clients across India and internationally — from local Vizag businesses to enterprises across the country. Every client relationship, every project, every line of code carries that same founding principle: trust first, always.

"When ₹40 lakhs of debt came, the family said: file for insolvency and walk away. Satheesh refused. 'I took those loans on my personal goodwill.' He paid every rupee back — cycling to meetings, cutting every corner except on integrity. That is the foundation F9 stands on today."

Leadership

Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu — Managing Director

Building technology. Empowering people. Creating the future.

Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu, Managing Director of F9 Info Technologies, is a technology entrepreneur, innovator, mentor, and visionary leader with a strong passion for transforming ideas into practical digital solutions.

His entrepreneurial journey began with just ₹2,000, a borrowed computer, and an unwavering determination to succeed. Over the years, that small beginning has grown into a trusted digital solutions company serving 600+ clients across India and delivering technology solutions to businesses across different industries.

Today, under his leadership, F9 Info Technologies is evolving into a next-generation technology and AI-focused company, exploring Artificial Intelligence, automation, modern software architecture, and emerging technologies to help businesses become more Agile, Scalable, and Efficient.

What makes Mr. Satheesh different as a leader is his approach to people. He does not believe in treating people simply as employees. He considers everyone a team member and works alongside the team as one among them. His simple and approachable nature creates an environment where people are encouraged to share ideas, experiment with new technologies, learn from failures, and grow together.

Mr. Satheesh continuously researches and explores new technologies, Artificial Intelligence, automation, open-source technologies, and emerging digital trends. His focus is not only on adopting technology but also on making it accessible and useful for businesses, developers, students, and entrepreneurs.

He also actively supports startups and emerging entrepreneurs, helping them transform their ideas into technology-driven products and scalable solutions. His vision is to create an ecosystem where innovative ideas can be developed faster, with the right technology, guidance, and resources.

One of his major initiatives is KDL-OS, an open-source technology platform being developed to simplify the process of building web applications and mobile applications. The vision behind KDL-OS is to make application development faster and easier — enabling users to build powerful digital products with minimal technical complexity, even through a click-based development experience.

From starting with a borrowed computer to leading a technology company into the era of AI and next-generation digital solutions, Mr. Satheesh Karnatakapu continues to believe that technology should not only build businesses — it should empower people, create opportunities, and solve real-world problems.

His Philosophy: "I don't want to build a company where people simply work. I want to build a team where people learn, create, grow, and build the future together."

His vision: Technology for Everyone. Innovation Without Limits.
