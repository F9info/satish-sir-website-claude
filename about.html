The Story

A Village Boy Who Never Stopped

Born in Munganda village, Konaseema, East Godavari — with a 12th standard education and one remarkable fact buried in his report card: a 99 out of 100 in Computers. Every other subject: 30 to 55 marks. That single score became the compass for everything.

His journey began not in a college but on a bus — the overnight Amalapuram to Rajahmundry bus, which he rode every single day for two years, collecting photo films and printing orders through the night. He later photographed from helicopters over Cairn Energy oil rigs and Deccan Aviation facilities — famous in his district. Then walked away because he wanted more.

A solar business of 100+ orders was destroyed overnight by a political appointment. He was left with ₹2,000. He borrowed a computer from his cousin and spent the next two years learning IT — 18 to 19 hours a day, alone, zero teacher. Bought 275 domains before building a single website. Started F9 Info Technologies in 2008.

When ₹40 lakhs of debt came, his family said: file for insolvency and walk away. He refused. "I took those loans on my personal goodwill." He rode a bicycle to client meetings. Made a handmade laptop-shaped portfolio. Built 600 clients. Paid every rupee back. Today, F9 celebrates 18 years — and Satheesh runs three organisations simultaneously.

Timeline

The Road From Munganda

1978 · Konaseema, East Godavari

Born in Munganda Village

A village near Amalapuram. 12th standard education. Scored 99/100 in Computers — the only subject that truly captured him. 30 to 55 in everything else.

1997–2000 · Amalapuram & Rajahmundry

The Photographer — Overnight Bus Runs

Photo lab collection centre, Amalapuram. Every day: 9 AM open → 7 PM bus to Rajahmundry → print overnight → sleep on chairs → 5 AM bus back → repeat. For two years. Later photographed from helicopters for Cairn Energy and Deccan Aviation.

Early 2000s · Asian Solar Systems

The Solar Dream — Destroyed by Politics

Traveled solo to Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai to learn the solar industry. MB Solar distributor for Vizag. Booked 100+ units — then a new political NETCAP chairman gave all contracts to a competitor overnight. Left with ₹2,000.

2003–2007 · Self-Teaching

18–19 Hours a Day. Zero Teacher.

Borrowed a computer from his cousin. Self-taught Java, web technologies, digital marketing, IT business models. 18 to 19 hours every day for 2 years. Bought 275+ domains across India before writing a single line of code.

2008 · F9 Info Technologies

"My Goal Is Very Big."

Launched F9 Info Technologies Pvt Ltd as a Private Limited company — deliberately. Built VizagPlus.com. ₹40 lakh debt when a US client cheated. Refused bankruptcy. Paid every rupee back. Rebuilt with a bicycle and a handmade laptop-shaped portfolio album. 500+ clients.

2015–2018 · 5 AM Club & Beach Campaign

1,000 Consecutive Days — Never Once Missed

1,000 consecutive days of 5 AM walks. Became President of the 5 AM Club. Then started "I Love Vizag Beach" — solo daily beach clean for 365 days. Transformed a littered viewpoint into Kalam View Point. 652 Sunday cleanups and counting.

2018 · Kalam Dream Labs

Empowering Dreamers

Founded Kalam Dream Labs Pvt Ltd. First student Kittu: UAV disaster management paper → won Delhi award → robotics expert in Australia — now Co-Director. 800+ rural students trained and placed in IT companies. 555 km walk to Amaravati — Safe Touch awareness for 2,00,000 students.

March 27, 2020 · COVID-19

"I Am Ready to Serve." — Vizag Volunteers Born

One WhatsApp status. Hundreds of calls in hours. Vizag Volunteers Association founded. 2 lakh meals. 150+ plasma donations. Went into hospitals when others would not. Lifted bodies. Saved lives. Today: 5,000 volunteers. 13 projects. Guinness World Record.

2025

Yogandhra 2025 Award

Honoured by the Government of Andhra Pradesh for outstanding social contribution. Mentioned last — because Satheesh never speaks about his awards.

The Real Story

Not for myself.
For Vizag.

Most people don't know what's actually behind all of this. Here it is — completely transparent, exactly as it is.

I am not married. I don't live this life for myself. Whatever I build — F9, Kalam Dream Labs, Vizag Volunteers — I am building it for the ecosystem, not for me. My only intention is to become part of Vizag's history. The same way Dr. Kalam became part of India's history.

🧭
Why I Keep Building
Whatever innovation, technology, money, or assets — none of it makes sense if it's not in the right hands. That is why I spend more time identifying and mentoring people than I do building products.

Some people stay one year. Some six months. Some three months. Some get frustrated with me. I don't mind. In my journey, only a few people can go forward with me. The ones who do are enough.
🌱
How I Mentor
I identify students and people from the next generation. I nurture them, give them opportunities, and test them — not to judge them, but to see if they're moving in the right direction for their own life.

Everybody has their own bandwidth. Some grow with me. Some grow elsewhere. Both outcomes are success. The intention is always to help someone find their path.
