Communities · Associations · Networks

Member of purpose.

Every community Satheesh belongs to is a reflection of how he chooses to invest his time — in people, in business, and in the city of Vizag.

8 Active Memberships across Business, Social & Innovation Networks
All Memberships
🏭
CII Visakhapatnam
Confederation of Indian Industry
India's premier business association connecting industry leaders, policymakers, and entrepreneurs. Satheesh participates in forums that shape Vizag's industrial and economic direction.
Member Visakhapatnam Chapter
🦁
Lions Club
Lions Club of Visakhapatnam
One of the world's largest service organisations with over 1.4 million members. Lions Club aligns with Satheesh's core belief: communities are built through consistent service, not one-time acts.
Member Visakhapatnam Chapter
🚀
TiE Vizag
The Indus Entrepreneurs
A global non-profit fostering entrepreneurship through mentoring, education, and networking. TiE Vizag connects founders, investors, and ecosystem builders — exactly the kind of network Satheesh helps build locally.
Member Vizag Chapter
💻
Google Developer Community
GDC Visakhapatnam
As a community organiser, Satheesh helps run events that bring developers, designers, and tech enthusiasts together in Vizag — building the city's technical talent pipeline from the ground up.
Community Organiser Visakhapatnam
🌱
Vizag Startup
Vizag Startup Ecosystem
A founding member of Vizag's startup community — one of the earliest voices championing an entrepreneurial ecosystem in the city. Helped lay the foundation for what is now a growing startup culture in Visakhapatnam.
Founding Member Visakhapatnam
🤝
BNI Amigos
Business Network International
BNI is the world's largest business referral network. The Amigos chapter in Vizag practices structured networking and relationship-based referrals — a model that aligns directly with how Satheesh builds trust in business: face to face, consistently.
Member BNI Amigos · Vizag
⚡
YI Vizag
Young Indians · CII Youth Wing
Young Indians is CII's youth wing focused on nation-building through entrepreneurship, leadership, and civic engagement. Satheesh's participation reflects his commitment to developing the next generation of Vizag's leaders.
Member Visakhapatnam Chapter
🏙️
JCI Port City
Junior Chamber International
JCI is a worldwide community of young active citizens working to create positive change. The Port City chapter in Vizag focuses on civic leadership and community development — areas Satheesh has committed his life to.
Member JCI Port City · Vizag

Community is not networking.

Satheesh doesn't join organisations to collect memberships. He joins to contribute — to bring ideas, connect people, and help the community grow stronger. Every forum is a place where the Vizag ecosystem gets a little better connected.

The common thread across all 8: people who want to build something, help someone, or make the city better. That's the only filter that matters.
