The Connection

Why these two frameworks together

Ikigai tells you what to pursue. Causing Incredible Performance tells you how to pursue it. One is the compass. The other is the engine.

🧭
Ikigai gives the direction
Without knowing your reason for being, all performance is just motion. Satheesh found his Ikigai — Vizag, people, technology, purpose — and every decision since has aligned to that center.
⚡
CIP gives the engine
Knowing your purpose is not enough. You must cause the outcome deliberately — rally people, declare goals, commit and account, reset your mind when needed, and simplify relentlessly.
🌆
Vizag is the proof
The result of applying both frameworks over 25 years: a technology company, a 5,000-person volunteer network, an innovation lab, and a vision for a city that becomes India's model — not by accident, but by design.
